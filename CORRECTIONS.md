# Inconsistencies in the Reference Handbook and their treatment

Citations break the global rule. In most of these cases the in text citation example is inconsistent with the bibliography.
This may be because they are presented as separate examples but it is unclear.
| Location                  | Inconsistency                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Treatment                                                                                           |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| p36:L9                    | **(Phung, et al., 2022)** has a comma before “et al.”  Every other 3+ author example is Au et al.                                                                                                                             | Treat as a typo and output **Phung et al.**                                                         |
| p37:L28 vs L30            | **(Elnokaly et al., 2023)**, but the list entry  has two authors (Elnokaly and Alqadi).  The global rule says 2 authors are cited **A and B**.                                                                                | Rule wins: **Elnokaly and Alqadi**                                                                  |
| p62:L29 vs L31            | **(Goult, 2023)**, but the entry has four authors,  so the rule gives **Goult et al.**.                                                                                                                                       | Rule wins                                                                                           |
| p77:L15 vs L17            | Mozart is **1989** in text and **1987** in the list.                                                                                                                                                                          | Use 1987, taken from the data                                                                       |
| p6:L24-27 vs p109:L22,L26 | National Gallery keeps “The” in the in-text citation. Later on,  "Open Innovation Team" drops it in text, though the list has  **Open Innovation Team, The and ….**  The p6 rule (“not in the in-text citation”) is ambiguous | Store the list form in **author** and the  in-text form in **shortauthor**, set  manually per entry |
| p40:L6 and P55:L15        | **Clinch, T. (2023)** and **Clinch, M. (2023)** both cite as  **(Clinch, 2023)**, which is ambiguous.                                                                                                                         | Leave **uniquename** off,  as the handbook does	                                                     |
