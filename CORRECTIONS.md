# Inconsistencies in the Reference Handbook and their treatment

Citations break the global rule. In most of these cases the in text citation example is inconsistent with the bibliography.
This may be because they are presented as separate examples but it is unclear.
| **#** | **Location**                               | **Inconsistency**                                                                                                                                                                                                              | **Treatment**                                                                                       |
|-------|--------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | Example p36:L9                             | **(Phung, et al., 2022)** has a comma before “et al.”  Every other 3+ author example is Au et al.                                                                                                                              | Treat as a typo and output **Phung et al.**                                                         |
| 2     | Example p37:L28 vs p37:L30                 | **(Elnokaly et al., 2023)**, but the list entry  has two authors (Elnokaly and Alqadi).  The global rule says 2 authors are cited **A and B**.                                                                                 | Rule wins: **Elnokaly and Alqadi**                                                                  |
| 3     | Example p62:L29 vs p62:L31                 | **(Goult, 2023)**, but the entry has four authors,  so the rule gives **Goult et al.**.                                                                                                                                        | Rule wins                                                                                           |
| 4     | Example p77:L15 vs p77:L17                 | Mozart is **1989** in text and **1987** in the list.                                                                                                                                                                           | Use 1987, taken from the data                                                                       |
| 5     | Example p6:L24-27 vs examples p109:L22,L26 | National Gallery keeps “The” in the in-text citation.
